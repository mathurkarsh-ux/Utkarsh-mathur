The Predictions

01
Agentic AI systems will autonomously execute multi-step professional workflows end-to-end
2025 – 2027 · Labour & Productivity
Confidence
95%
Near-term ↓
Technical Rationale
Current frontier models (Claude 3.5+, GPT-4o) already demonstrate reliable tool use, multi-step reasoning, and context retention across long tasks. The architectural primitives — function calling, memory, browser control — are production-ready. The limiting factor has shifted from capability to reliability and deployment infrastructure, both of which are compressing rapidly. Anthropic's Computer Use, OpenAI's Operator, and Google's Project Mariner are early commercial instantiations of this trajectory.
Leading Signals
  • Anthropic Claude Computer Use in public beta — browser, code, file manipulation
  • OpenAI Operator completing real e-commerce and travel workflows autonomously
  • Enterprise deployment of AI agents in legal document review, financial analysis
  • SWE-bench scores exceeding 50% — AI solving real GitHub issues autonomously
Knowledge Work Legal Finance Software Development
02
AI will compress drug discovery timelines from decades to under five years
2026 – 2030 · Life Sciences
Confidence
90%
Science ↓
Technical Rationale
AlphaFold2 and AlphaFold3 effectively solved the protein structure prediction problem that took structural biology 50 years. The same deep learning paradigm is now being applied to protein-ligand binding prediction, molecular dynamics simulation, and ADMET property prediction. Companies like Isomorphic Labs, Recursion Pharmaceuticals, and Insilico Medicine are moving candidates through preclinical phases at 10x speed with significantly lower attrition rates. The bottleneck shifts to clinical trial execution — a regulatory, not computational, constraint.
Leading Signals
  • AlphaFold3 predicts protein-ligand, protein-DNA, protein-RNA interactions
  • Insilico Medicine's INS018_055 — first AI-designed drug in Phase II clinical trials
  • Recursion processed 2.5PB of biological data to identify novel drug candidates
  • Isomorphic Labs partnerships with Eli Lilly and Novartis worth $3B+
Oncology Rare Diseases Antibiotic Resistance
03
Multimodal AI will become the primary human-computer interface, displacing touch and keyboard for most interactions
2026 – 2029 · HCI
Confidence
85%
Interface ↓
Technical Rationale
GPT-4o's real-time voice, vision, and reasoning capabilities demonstrated in May 2024 represent the convergence of audio, visual, and language modalities into a unified model. Latency has dropped from seconds to sub-200ms — the threshold for natural conversation. Combined with AR/VR form factors (Apple Vision Pro, Meta Ray-Bans), the contextual awareness required for ambient computing is achievable within this decade. The keyboard survives for precision input; touch survives for direct manipulation — but ambient voice and vision become the dominant interaction paradigm.
Leading Signals
  • GPT-4o real-time API — sub-320ms audio response latency
  • Apple Intelligence integrating Siri with frontier models at OS level
  • Meta AI glasses with real-time vision and voice — 30M+ units shipped
  • Google Gemini Live — continuous multimodal conversation on Android
Consumer Devices Enterprise Software Accessibility
04
AI will achieve superhuman performance across all standard medical diagnostic benchmarks
2025 – 2027 · Healthcare
Confidence
95%
Healthcare ↓
Technical Rationale
AI diagnostic performance has already exceeded specialist-level accuracy in radiology (chest X-ray, mammography, retinal imaging), dermatology, and pathology on benchmark datasets. The remaining gap is in clinical reasoning under uncertainty — integrating patient history, comorbidities, and ambiguous presentation into a differential diagnosis. Med-PaLM 2, GPT-4 on USMLE Step 3, and Google's AMIE system achieving expert physician performance on diagnostic conversations represent the closing of this gap. The transition barrier is regulatory approval and liability frameworks, not capability.
Leading Signals
  • Google's AMIE outperformed primary care physicians on diagnostic accuracy in blinded trials
  • GPT-4 scores 87th percentile on USMLE — well above passing threshold
  • FDA cleared 692 AI-enabled medical devices as of 2023 — accelerating
  • DeepMind's AlphaFold cited in Nobel Prize for Chemistry 2024
Radiology Pathology Primary Care Rare Disease
05
Frontier AI labs will demonstrate systems capable of conducting original scientific research autonomously
2027 – 2030 · Research
Confidence
80%
Science ↓
Technical Rationale
AI2's Semantic Scholar, DeepMind's GNoME (discovered 2.2M new crystal structures), and Google's FunSearch (discovered new mathematical theorems) demonstrate that AI can already operate at the frontier of narrow scientific domains. The next step is closing the loop — hypothesis generation, experimental design, result interpretation, and iterative refinement without human intervention at each stage. The key bottleneck is long-horizon reasoning and the ability to recognise meaningful novelty versus noise. Scaling laws suggest this is a solvable capability gap within current architectural paradigms.
Leading Signals
  • DeepMind GNoME — 2.2M crystal structures, 736 synthesised and validated
  • Google FunSearch — discovered new solutions to cap set and bin packing problems
  • AlphaGeometry solved 25/30 IMO geometry problems at gold medallist level
  • Sakana AI's "AI Scientist" — autonomous paper ideation, execution, writing
Materials Science Mathematics Physics
06
AI-generated synthetic media will require mandatory provenance infrastructure to remain trusted
2025 – 2027 · Governance
Confidence
90%
Policy ↓
Technical Rationale
Sora, Kling, and Runway Gen-3 have reduced the cost and skill barrier for photorealistic video synthesis to near-zero. The epistemological problem — how does any viewer verify the provenance of any media? — cannot be solved through detection alone (adversarial arms race). The solution converges on cryptographic content credentials embedded at capture (C2PA standard, adopted by Adobe, Google, Microsoft, Qualcomm), combined with platform-level disclosure requirements. EU AI Act Article 50 mandates disclosure for synthetic media. US and UK legislation is following. This prediction is less about if and more about the timeline to ubiquitous deployment.
Leading Signals
  • C2PA standard adopted by Adobe, Google, Microsoft, Truepic, Qualcomm
  • EU AI Act — mandatory disclosure requirements for AI-generated content
  • Meta, YouTube, TikTok deploying AI content labelling infrastructure
  • Deepfake incidents in 2024 US election cycle accelerating regulatory response
Elections Journalism Legal Evidence
07
Personalised AI tutors will demonstrably outperform classroom instruction for most learning objectives
2026 – 2030 · Education
Confidence
85%
Education ↓
Technical Rationale
Bloom's 2 Sigma problem — established in 1984 — demonstrated that one-on-one tutoring produces learning outcomes 2 standard deviations better than classroom instruction. The constraint was cost: personalised tutoring at scale was economically impossible. LLMs resolve the cost constraint entirely. Khanmigo, Synthesis, and emerging AI tutoring platforms already demonstrate adaptive pacing, Socratic questioning, and real-time misconception detection. The remaining challenges are motivation, accountability, and social learning — none of which are fundamental barriers to AI-augmented education systems.
Leading Signals
  • Khan Academy's Khanmigo — Socratic AI tutor deployed to millions of students
  • Duolingo Max — AI conversation practice with near-native feedback
  • MIT study: students using AI tutors learned material 2x faster vs. traditional instruction
  • Synthesis AI tutor — measurable gains in mathematical reasoning for K-12
K-12 Higher Education Professional Development
08
AI will accelerate materials discovery for next-generation energy storage and semiconductor applications
2026 – 2032 · Energy
Confidence
80%
Energy ↓
Technical Rationale
The materials discovery pipeline — synthesise candidate, characterise properties, iterate — has historically required years per candidate. Graph neural networks trained on materials databases (Materials Project, ICSD) can now predict formation energy, band gap, and stability of novel compounds before synthesis. Microsoft's MatterGen generates novel materials with targeted properties. For batteries, the challenge is finding solid-state electrolytes with high ionic conductivity and electrochemical stability — a multi-objective optimisation problem well-suited to ML. Microsoft claims its AI-assisted discovery of a new solid electrolyte reduced the exploration space from 32M candidates to 18 in weeks.
Leading Signals
  • Microsoft Azure Quantum Elements — solid electrolyte discovery in weeks, not years
  • DeepMind GNoME predicts thermodynamic stability of 2.2M inorganic crystals
  • TSMC and ASML using ML for EUV lithography process optimisation
  • Google TPU v5 chip design partially optimised by reinforcement learning
Battery Technology Semiconductors Solar Cells
09
The gap between frontier AI capability and open-source capability will narrow to under 12 months
2025 – 2027 · Industry
Confidence
75%
Industry ↓
Technical Rationale
The release of Llama 2 in July 2023 and Llama 3 in April 2024 demonstrated Meta's willingness to release near-frontier weights publicly. DeepSeek R1 in January 2026 released a model matching OpenAI o1 performance at a fraction of compute cost, with full weights open. The architectural innovations driving capability — mixture-of-experts, chain-of-thought, reinforcement learning from human feedback — are now public knowledge. The moat for frontier labs increasingly depends on data quality, RLHF infrastructure, and deployment scale — not architecture secrecy. The capability gap narrows as algorithmic efficiency improvements diffuse through open research.
Leading Signals
  • DeepSeek R1 — matches OpenAI o1 on benchmarks, fully open weights, $6M training cost
  • Llama 3.1 405B — competitive with GPT-4 class models, open source
  • Mistral, Qwen, Gemma — competitive open models from multiple labs
  • Hugging Face Open LLM Leaderboard tracking rapid capability convergence
Commoditisation Enterprise Deployment AI Safety
10
AI alignment and interpretability will become foundational engineering requirements, not optional research
2026 – 2030 · Safety
Confidence
70%
Safety ↓
Technical Rationale
As AI systems are deployed in high-stakes domains — medical diagnosis, legal decisions, financial trading, autonomous vehicles — the question of why a system produced a specific output becomes legally and operationally critical. Anthropic's mechanistic interpretability research (identifying circuits, features, and attention patterns), OpenAI's superalignment team (before its restructuring), and DeepMind's safety work represent significant institutional investment. EU AI Act's high-risk AI provisions mandate explainability and human oversight for consequential decisions. The market signal is clear: interpretability tooling will be required for enterprise AI deployment in regulated industries, creating commercial pressure aligned with safety research objectives.
Leading Signals
  • Anthropic's interpretability — mapping features in Claude to human-readable concepts
  • EU AI Act classifying medical, legal, and critical infrastructure AI as high-risk
  • NIST AI Risk Management Framework adopted as enterprise standard
  • US Executive Order on AI requiring safety evaluations for frontier models
