The Problem

😩 Before
20 minutes of indecision
Every evening — what's for dinner? Cook arrives needing instructions. Nobody has ordered groceries. End up browsing Netflix for 20 minutes. Can't decide. Watch YouTube. Order Swiggy. Again.
✅ After
5pm. Everything decided.
A message arrives at 5pm. Dinner suggestion with exact grocery list. Four personalised watch picks — one for me, one for wife, one together. Cook gets instructions. Done. No decisions needed.

How It Works

1
⏰ 5pm — Zapier wakes up
A scheduled trigger fires every day at exactly 5pm India time. No manual intervention. Ever.
2
📊 Reads Google Sheets memory
Checks Watch History and Food History tabs — so it never repeats what we watched or ate in the last 3 days.
3
🧠 AI generates the brief
GPT-4o mini reads our taste profiles — my love for action/war/spy, wife's love for horror/thriller — and generates personalised picks from Netflix, Prime, Hotstar, ZEE5, SonyLIV.
4
📱 Sends to Telegram + Gmail
KarshFamilyBot delivers the full brief to our family Telegram group. Gmail backup for when away at sea.
5
💬 We reply — agent learns
Reply W1/W2/Made/Ordered after dinner. Agent logs it. Recommendations get smarter every single day.
Schedule by Zapier Formatter by Zapier AI by Zapier (GPT-4o mini) Telegram Bot API Gmail Google Sheets
